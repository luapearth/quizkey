How it works

Earn it. Play. Lock again.

No arguments, no nagging. The app does the timekeeping.

01

Pick the apps that need earning

Tap the padlock next to an app. It stays locked until your child earns minutes.

02

Take a quiz

Every correct answer adds minutes, with confetti. A wrong answer shows the right one, kindly.

03

Play, then it locks again

Minutes count down while they play. At zero: "Time's up!" and the apps lock again.

Do I have to write every question myself?

No. The question bank has ready-made questions in Math, Spelling, General knowledge and the Philippines. You can also fill your own categories from a spreadsheet: save QuizKey's CSV template, fill it in Google Sheets, Excel or Numbers (one question per row, with the right answer and 1 to 3 wrong ones), download it as CSV and import it. Then add a whole category at once, or swipe through it one question at a time.

Does it need the internet?

No. QuizKey works fully offline. On Android it doesn't even request internet access.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 8.0 or newer, and iPhones running iOS 16 or newer. iPhone beta testing on TestFlight starts soon; ask for an invite.

How does QuizKey lock apps on iPhone?

With Apple's Screen Time. The child's iPhone needs to be signed in to a child account in Family Sharing, and you approve QuizKey once with your Apple ID. Then you pick the apps to lock in Apple's own picker. iOS never tells QuizKey which apps you picked.

Android: what's a work profile, and why does QuizKey use one?

It's a separate, badged set of apps that Android lets one app manage. QuizKey uses it so locked apps really stay locked. Your child's games go in the work profile; QuizKey can't see or change anything outside it.

Android: can it lock apps outside the work profile?

No. Android keeps the personal side private, so only apps in the work profile (the ones with the briefcase badge) can be locked.

My phone already has a work profile. Can I use QuizKey?

A phone can have only one work profile, so the strong lock won't be available on a phone that already has one (for example, from an employer). The quizzes and minutes still work.

Android: how do I add my child's games?

In Parent mode, tap Open Play Store and install them in the work profile. QuizKey locks new apps there automatically.

Is it free?

QuizKey is in early testing and free to try on Android and iPhone. Want in? Join the beta.

How do I delete everything?

On Android, uninstall QuizKey or clear its storage in Settings; if you set up the strong lock, remove the work profile too. On iPhone, a parent turns QuizKey off in Settings → Screen Time, then deletes the app. We hold no data on our side.
