QuizKey for Android and iPhone

Our privacy policy.

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Apps: QuizKey for Android (com.luapearth.quizkey) and QuizKey for iPhone (App Store: “QuizKey – Screen Time Quiz”)

The short version

  • QuizKey does not collect any personal information. Nothing you or your child enter is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • QuizKey doesn't use the internet. On Android it doesn't even ask for permission to; on iPhone it makes no network connections of its own.
  • Questions, photos, minutes and settings are stored only on the device, and are deleted when you delete the app. A spreadsheet you import is read once and not uploaded.
  • No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Who this policy is for

QuizKey is a screen-time app for families, on Android and on iPhone. A parent chooses which apps are locked, writes quiz questions and sets the rules; a child answers questions to earn minutes on those apps. This policy explains what the app stores, where it stays and what control you have.

What the app stores on your device

To work, QuizKey keeps the following in its private app storage on the phone:

InformationWhyWhere
Quiz questions, answers and optional photos a parent addsTo show them to the child in quizzesOn the device only
The question bank: categories, the questions waiting in them, and which were added or skippedSo a parent can add them to the quiz questions all at once or one by oneOn the device only
Minutes earned and used, with timesTo keep the child's balance and show today's activityOn the device only

Photos. When a parent adds a photo to a question, QuizKey makes its own smaller copy in its private storage. It reads only the photo the parent picks (through the system photo picker) or takes with the camera.

Spreadsheet (CSV) files. A parent can fill a question-bank category from a CSV file. QuizKey reads only the file the parent picks in the system file picker, once, when they tap Import, and copies the questions into its private storage. It doesn't keep, change or upload the file, and it doesn't ask for permission to see your other files. The CSV template is saved or shared only where the parent chooses. The ready-made question banks come built into the app; nothing is downloaded.

Answers. QuizKey counts right answers to award minutes. It does not build profiles of your child or send results anywhere.

None of this is transmitted to us, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Backups. On Android, QuizKey turns off automatic app backup, so this data is not copied to cloud backups. On iPhone, like other apps' data, it is included in your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer) if you have one turned on. That backup belongs to your Apple account; we never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. QuizKey has no account and no sign-in, and it doesn't send information off the device: it doesn't use the internet. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share any data.

Children

QuizKey is designed for families, and children use it to take quizzes. Because QuizKey does not collect, transmit or share personal information, no personal information from children reaches us or any third party. Parents control the content (they write or choose the questions) and the settings (behind a parent PIN). Questions written by a family are seen only on that family's device.

Permissions and device controls

On Android

QuizKey asks for only what it needs:

  • Work profile / device administration. To lock the apps a parent chooses, QuizKey becomes the manager of a separate work profile (or of a dedicated child's phone) through Android's own setup, which the parent confirms. It uses this only to pause and un-pause the apps the parent selects, and to show a short message when a locked app is opened. It does not read messages, browsing, location or the contents of other apps. In a work profile, it cannot see or control the personal side of the phone.
  • Notifications and a foreground service. These show the countdown ("12:34 left") while a child uses earned minutes, and keep the timer accurate.
  • Run at start-up. After the phone restarts, QuizKey makes sure locked apps are locked again.

On iPhone

  • Screen Time (Apple's Family Controls). On the child's iPhone, which is signed in to a child account in Family Sharing, a parent approves QuizKey's Screen Time access with their own Apple ID. QuizKey uses it only to lock the apps the parent picks, show QuizKey's message when a locked app is opened, and lock the apps again when earned minutes run out. The parent picks apps in Apple's own app picker, and iOS does not tell QuizKey which apps they are: QuizKey only receives a private reference it can lock and unlock. QuizKey does not receive Screen Time usage data, and it cannot see messages, browsing, location or what your child does in other apps.
  • Camera (asked only when a parent taps Take photo): to take a photo for a quiz question.
  • QuizKey and its two small helper parts (the screen shown over a locked app, and the timer that locks apps again) share the information above through a private storage area on the iPhone that only they can use.

App stores

QuizKey is distributed through Google Play, and on iPhone through Apple's App Store and TestFlight. Google and Apple may collect information when you download or update apps under their own privacy policies. QuizKey itself does not send anything to Google or Apple.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete individual questions any time in Parent mode → Questions, and your own categories (with the questions still waiting in them) in Parent mode → Question bank.
  • Delete everything on Android: uninstall QuizKey (Settings → Apps → QuizKey → Uninstall) or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → QuizKey → Storage → Clear storage). If you set up the strong lock, remove the work profile in Settings to remove the QuizKey inside it as well.
  • Delete everything on iPhone: delete the QuizKey app. Because a parent approved Screen Time access, your child can't delete it; a parent first turns QuizKey off in Settings → Screen Time (iOS may ask for the parent's approval), then deletes it. To stop locking without deleting, turn its Screen Time access off the same way. Copies in your iPhone's own backups are removed as those backups are replaced or deleted.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side. You can still contact us with any questions.

Changes to this policy

If QuizKey changes how it handles data, for example by adding optional syncing between a parent's and a child's phone, we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released, and explain the change in the app.
