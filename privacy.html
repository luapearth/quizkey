QuizKey for Android

Our privacy policy.

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · App: QuizKey for Android (com.luapearth.quizkey)

The short version

  • QuizKey does not collect any personal information. Nothing you or your child enter is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The app has no internet access: it doesn't ask Android for permission to use the network.
  • Questions, photos, minutes and settings are stored only on the device, and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • No ads, no accounts, no analytics, no tracking.

Who this policy is for

QuizKey is a screen-time app for families. A parent chooses which apps are locked, writes quiz questions and sets the rules; a child answers questions to earn minutes on those apps. This policy explains what the app stores, where it stays and what control you have.

What the app stores on your device

To work, QuizKey keeps the following in its private app storage on the phone:

InformationWhyWhere
Quiz questions, answers and optional photos a parent addsTo show them to the child in quizzesOn the device only
Minutes earned and used, with timesTo keep the child's balance and show today's activityOn the device only
Rules: minutes per answer, questions per quiz, daily limitTo apply the parent's settingsOn the device only
The parent PIN, stored only as a one-way scrambled code (salted hash)To keep Parent mode for parentsOn the device only
Which apps the parent chose to lockTo lock and unlock themOn the device only
Names of apps on the phone that open from the home screenSo the parent can choose which apps to lockOn the device only

Photos. When a parent adds a photo to a question, QuizKey makes its own smaller copy in its private storage. It reads only the photo the parent picks (through Android's photo picker) or takes with the camera app.

Answers. QuizKey counts right answers to award minutes. It does not build profiles of your child or send results anywhere.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage and is not included in device backups.

What we collect

Nothing. QuizKey has no account and no sign-in, and it cannot send information off the device: the app doesn't have internet access. We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share any data.

Children

QuizKey is designed for families, and children use it to take quizzes. Because QuizKey does not collect, transmit or share personal information, no personal information from children reaches us or any third party. Parents control the content (they write the questions) and the settings (behind a parent PIN). Questions written by a family are seen only on that family's device.

Permissions and device controls

  • Work profile / device administration. To lock the apps a parent chooses, QuizKey becomes the manager of a separate work profile (or of a dedicated child's phone) through Android's own setup, which the parent confirms. It uses this only to pause and un-pause the apps the parent selects, and to show a short message when a locked app is opened. It does not read messages, browsing, location or the contents of other apps. In a work profile, it cannot see or control the personal side of the phone.
  • Notifications and a foreground service. These show the countdown ("12:34 left") while a child uses earned minutes, and keep the timer accurate.
  • Run at start-up. After the phone restarts, QuizKey makes sure locked apps are locked again.

Services provided by Google

QuizKey is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information when you download or update apps under its own privacy policy. QuizKey itself does not send anything to Google.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete individual questions any time in Parent mode → Questions.
  • To delete everything, uninstall QuizKey (Settings → Apps → QuizKey → Uninstall) or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → QuizKey → Storage → Clear storage).
  • If you set up the strong lock, remove the work profile in Settings to remove the QuizKey inside it as well.
  • We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If QuizKey changes how it handles data, for example by adding optional syncing between a parent's and a child's phone, we will update this page and the effective date before that change reaches the app.
